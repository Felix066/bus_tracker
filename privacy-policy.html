Privacy Policy & Data Security Terms

Last Updated: August 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to BusTrack. This Privacy Policy outlines our strict practices regarding the collection, use, and security of your personal data. By accessing our services, you expressly consent to this policy.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We collect location data (GPS coordinates), name, and student/driver identifiers to facilitate real-time bus tracking. This data is strictly used for operational routing and safety purposes within the application.

3. Location Tracking & Background Processing

For drivers and students, the application requires persistent or background location access to provide accurate Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA) predictions and proximity notifications. You may opt out of location sharing at any time via device settings, though this will limit application functionality.

4. Data Security & Protection

We employ industry-leading security protocols to protect your data against unauthorized access, SQL injection, brute force, and other software attacks. Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS, and sensitive data is secured at rest. Our systems utilize Row-Level Security (RLS) policies to ensure users can only access their authorized data.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties. Data is only accessible to authorized college administrators and the secure database infrastructure required to run the service.

6. User Rights & Consent

By creating an account and logging in, you explicitly consent to these terms. You have the right to request deletion of your data by contacting your campus administrator.
