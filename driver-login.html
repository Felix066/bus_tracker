Permissions & Data Management

Permissions & Data Management

Where Is My Bus requires certain permissions to provide core features like live bus tracking and SOS alerts. We value your privacy and are completely transparent about how your data is used.

Location Access (Background)

Prominent Disclosure: Where Is My Bus collects location data to enable real-time tracking of the bus, provide ETAs to students, and update the live map even when the app is closed or not in use during an active trip.

Data Management

Location data is securely transmitted over HTTPS and is only used temporarily to display the bus on the map. Data is not sold to third parties. You can manage your permissions in your device settings.
